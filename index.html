Welcome to Penrose Machining

Gene Penrose began Penrose Machining, Inc. in his garage back in 1979 with a turret lathe. With no salesman on the road, Gene built his business by word of mouth and developed a reputation for quality that we still maintain today.

Our goal at Penrose Machining, Inc is to supply our customers with high quality machined parts in a timely manner. We pride ourselves in our flexibility to work with our customers to meet their on going changes, business progress and future development.

We take a personal interest in each job and we offer competitive pricing, flexible turn-around time, and personal service.

Here at Penrose Machining we provide a variety of services and equipment to fit your needs whether you are a small business of 2 or 3 people or a large corporation that has a large workforce and multiple locations.
