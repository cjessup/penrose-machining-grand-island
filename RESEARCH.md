# Penrose Machining redesign research

## Line of business (from the live site)

Penrose Machining, Inc. is a Grand Island, Nebraska **job shop**. It produces custom machined parts for industrial, agricultural, and automotive applications. The live capabilities pages cover turning (CNC lathes), screw machines (including Citizen Swiss), milling (manual mills and vertical machining centers), sawing, hobbing/shaping, broaching, grinding, drilling, and in-house quality inspection. Home and contact pages state **ISO 9001:2015 Certified**.

Confirmed on the live contact page, and repeated only where the site states them:

The about page says Gene Penrose started the company in his garage in **1979** with a turret lathe, the current facility dates to **1990**, the plant is **36,000 square feet**, and the shop averages **40 employees**. Hours are not published on the live site, so this demo does not list hours.

## Peer sites and ideas borrowed

Layout and structure only. No text, photos, or branding copied.

### Owens Industries — https://owensind.com/

Precision CNC machine shop (milling, turning, EDM). Ideas used:

- Put the process list directly under the opening statement so a buyer sees what the shop runs before the history.
- State the ISO registration as an early trust line, not only in the footer.
- Keep each machine list under its process instead of one long equipment dump.
- Repeat a quote request beside the technical detail.

### Reata Engineering & Machine Works — https://reataeng.com/

Contract CNC machine shop. Ideas used:

- Keep a request-a-quote action in the header area on every page.
- Tell the founder story as a short history next to a real plant photograph, then the facility facts.
- Pair a process with the photograph that belongs to it.
- Make service names a scannable set that opens the longer description.

### Pioneer Service Inc. — https://pioneerserviceinc.com/

Production CNC Swiss and grinding shop. Ideas used:

- Treat core capabilities as compact labeled entries, with capacity notes kept tight under each process (chips, not large cards).
- Close the capabilities page with a plain “send the print / request a quote” band.
- Repeat address and phone in the footer so a buyer can act without hunting.
